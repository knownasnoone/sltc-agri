VitaBone is a calcium-enriched functional cracker made with Goldstripe Sardinella fish bone powder and Moringa leaf powder. It provides calcium, phosphorus, vitamins, minerals, dietary fibre, and antioxidants. Designed as a convenient ready-to-eat cracker, VitaBone combines nutrition and sustainability by converting fish-processing by-products into a value-added food product with a crispy texture and appealing colour.
