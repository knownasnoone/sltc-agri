Our fruit leather products are innovative, fruitbased snack options made using locally available fruits. Watermelon & Dragon Fruit Fruit Leather combines the refreshing taste and attractive natural colours of two tropical fruits, while Wood Apple Fruit Leather offers a unique sweet and tangy flavour. These products provide a convenient and enjoyable way to consume fruit in a different form. They are developed to add value to local fruits while offering consumers a tasty, natural, and appealing snack alternative suitable for different age groups.
