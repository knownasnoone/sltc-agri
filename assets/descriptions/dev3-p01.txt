CassaTreat Edible Cup is an innovative, gluten-free, cassava-based edible cup designed to serve ice cream, yogurt, pudding, fruit salad and other desserts. Made with cassava flour, corn starch, egg, milk, butter and carefully selected ingredients, it offers a light, crisp texture and pleasant vanilla flavour. After enjoying the filling, consumers can eat the cup, helping reduce waste from disposable serving containers. Convenient, tasty and easy to use, CassaTreat combines food innovation, sustainability and value-added cassava utilization to create a fun dessert experience for consumers of all ages.
