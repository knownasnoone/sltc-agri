NutriVeg Leather is a colorful, ready-to-eat fruit and vegetable snack made from beetroot, carrot, pumpkin, apple puree, and glucose syrup. It has a soft, chewy texture and provides dietary fiber, vitamins, minerals, antioxidants, and natural pigments without artificial colors. Its unique feature is that one pack contains four naturally colored vegetable leathers, offering variety and attractive appearance. Unlike many conventional snacks, NutriVeg Leather combines locally available fruits and vegetables into a nutritious, convenient, and enjoyable snack suitable for children, students, athletes, and health-conscious consumers.
